I'm Chitransh, a senior product designer. I design enterprise products where the screen is dense and the stakes are high: cybersecurity, healthcare, fintech and more. The base is old-school product design: research, flows and systems. On top of that I use agentic and design engineering to prototype and ship what I design.

What I bring

Five things a team gets on day one, each with the project that proves it.

01
Calm screens
I turn dense security and clinical data into a clear hierarchy, so the next action is obvious. On Qualys VMDR, analysts triage vulnerabilities about twice as fast.
02
Systems that hold
Worked on 10+ design systems and built 3 from scratch. I founded and built Quad with the early Qualys team (20+ modules, about 60% fewer UI inconsistencies), built one for Northwell, and worked on IBM's design systems.
03
AI people trust
Agent workflows where a person can see the reasoning, correct it and stay in control. On a clinical review flow, reviewers correct AI drafts section by section and drafting time fell about 50%. On IBM CPQ, an AI check cut manual rework about 30%.
04
Design that ships
A product designer first. I use Claude and Cursor as tools: to prototype an idea, to understand the build tradeoffs, and to take design work from Figma into a React repository. The research, flows and systems underneath are still done the old way.
05
A team that grows
I lead a team of six designers, mentor through critique and pairing, and keep product, engineering and business on one roadmap.

Small talk

The short ones are here. For anything else, ask the moon.

How did you get into design?

I studied both sides of it: a Bachelor of Fine Arts at Jiwaji University in Gwalior and a Bachelor's in Computer Science at DAVV. Then a master's in Communication Design at RMIT. Product design is where drawing and logic meet.

What's your main thing?

Enterprise products where the screen is dense and a wrong click costs something: security consoles, clinical data, financial tools. I make the important thing obvious and build the design system that keeps it that way.

Where are you based?

Pune, India. I work across time zones with global clients.

How can I get in touch?

Email trivedichitransh12@gmail.com or message me on LinkedIn. Tell me what you're building and where it's hard.
